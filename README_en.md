## 🔬 Research

### Image Classification with Reinforcement Learning

I study how to train image recognition models using rewards based on classification outcomes.
I'm interested in how reward design and the number of classes affect accuracy and learning behavior.

### Guqin Tablature Recognition and Music Reconstruction

I developed a system that reads tablature for the traditional Chinese guqin from images and converts it into playable music data.
The research combines image recognition and generative AI to supplement performance information omitted from the tablature, with the aim of supporting cultural preservation and music learning.

### Facial Expression Recognition with Face and Scene Context

I studied models that estimate emotions using both facial expressions and the surrounding scene.
I compared different ways to combine information from the face and background.

## 🌏 Projects

### [SP!ED2026 — Food Freshness Assessment and Refrigerator Turntable](https://ire-asia.org/ire/spied/)

In a team of students from Japan, China, and South Korea, we developed a product that assesses food freshness with AI and brings priority items to the front of a refrigerator.
We focused on the problems of overlooked and hard-to-reach food, and our team received a Gold Award.

### [SP!ED2025 — IoT App for Safer Pet Walks](https://ire-asia.org/ire/spied/index.php/spied2025/)

In a team of students from Japan, China, and South Korea, we developed an app that uses ground temperature to alert users to burn risks during pet walks.
I mainly handled frontend development, and our team received a Silver Award.
